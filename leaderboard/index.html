How ranking works

  • Designs are ranked by total cycles, summed over every competition trace. Lower is better.
  • To be ranked, a design must pass every trace, meet timing (slack ≥ 0) and stay under the area limit.
  • Only your most recent graded commit counts. If it breaks, you drop off until a later run passes.
  • The board updates automatically after each autograder run.
